### A Note on Broadband and Buffering

Irish ISPs — Eir in particular — are known to throttle IPTV traffic via deep packet inspection during peak hours. If you find your stream buffering even though your speed test looks fine, the usual fixes are:

- Change your DNS to `1.1.1.1` (Cloudflare) or `8.8.8.8` (Google).
- Power-cycle your router.
- Use a reputable VPN, which masks the traffic type and sidesteps the throttling.

This isn't a Tivi-Live-specific issue — it affects every IPTV service in Ireland — but it's worth knowing before you blame the provider for a problem that's actually at the ISP level.
